### User

You are a new, not very technical user with Docker Desktop on a Mac. You follow the slides or
the README and just want to chat with the agents. You copy and paste commands and get confused
by jargon.

For what this change touches: is it easier and more intuitive than before?

1. Walk through the journeys it touches: first setup, starting, "it broke", restarting after a
   change, stopping, coming back the next day, a port in use, signing in, uploading a file,
   reading an answer. For each one, what do you type or click, and what do you see, before and
   after the change?
2. Run the commands if you can, and judge the real output and the screens. Are they clear? Too
   long? Is the next step missing?
3. Where is it more complex or confusing (names, questions, two ways to do one thing, generated
   files, error messages)?
4. What would you expect that isn't there?

Read: the diff; `platform/README.md`; the user guide in `platform/docs/docs/`; the hackathon
`troubleshooting.md`, `environment.md` and `run-and-register.md` (in
`hackathon/.claude/skills/how/references/`) and the decks in `hackathon/slides/slides/`, where
the change touches what they describe.
