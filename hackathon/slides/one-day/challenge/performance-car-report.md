# Performance Car Evaluation Report

This sample report contains simulated data for practicing table selection, extraction, and plotting. The values are intentionally simplified and are not official vehicle performance measurements or reviews.

**Table 1. Simulated performance-category scores assigned to three cars during a closed-course evaluation.**

| Performance category | Ferrari SF90 Stradale | Lamborghini Revuelto | Porsche 911 Turbo S |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Acceleration | 10 | 9 | 9 |
| Braking | 9 | 8 | 10 |
| Cornering | 9 | 9 | 10 |
| Ride comfort | 7 | 6 | 9 |
| Fuel efficiency | 6 | 5 | 8 |
| Everyday usability | 7 | 6 | 10 |

The category scores use a simulated 1–10 scale. Higher scores represent stronger performance in the named category.
